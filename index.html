01

What happens

Parker is four. He doesn't do what anyone expects him to do — not at daycare, not at the doctor, not at home.

His parents are trying everything. Daycare, doctors, babysitters, a stack of forms. None of it explains their kid.

Then one afternoon Parker gets on his scooter and goes.

The rest of the film goes with him. Alleys, the pool, the hill at the park, strangers, the whole neighbourhood. And the further he gets, the more we see it his way — light, noise, heat, all of it turned up.

The kid we're looking for

Five or six, playing four. Takes direction. Doesn't mind doing a thing twice. Fine around strangers. Good at pretending.

That's the whole list. No acting experience needed. No background of any kind needed.

The commitment is real, but it's gentler than a normal shoot — short days spread across summer 2027, a crew of two or three, and you there the entire time. Nobody is asking a four-year-old to carry a scene.

There's an honorarium for your kid's time. The amount rides on the grant, so we can't put a number on it yet.

Sounds like your family? Write to us up top.

03

How it gets made

Most of Scooterland is built out of 35mm photographs instead of filmed scenes. We shoot stills in the neighbourhood, and the moving images get generated from those.

The photographs aren't only raw material. They're the work too, and they'll be shown as photographs.

One good still costs a fraction of what a movie shot costs. Whether a still can really become a piece of a moving film is the thing we most want to find out.

04

Who else we need

  • Cinematographer / photographer — the film is photographed, not shot.
  • Composer / sound designer — sound carries most of what's happening in Parker's head.
  • Editor — where the cut and the movement get decided at the same time.
  • Producer — small production, real stake, ownership model still open.
  • Nate and Flora — Parker's parents. Two adult leads.

Lived any of this yourself, as a parent or as a neurodivergent person or through your work? That counts for a lot here, and it's expected to change the script.

Not on the list? Write anyway.
